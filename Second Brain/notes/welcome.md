# Welcome to Your Second Brain

## Summary

> This is an example note showing how to use the Second Brain. Copy `note-template.md` to create your own.

## Notes

- Every note is a plain markdown file in the `notes/` folder.
- Use front matter (the block at the top between `---`) to track metadata.
- Keep notes atomic — one idea per file.

## Details

Your Second Brain is a place to offload thoughts so you don't have to hold
everything in your head. Capture quickly, then refine over time. The goal is
a network of small, linked notes that grow more valuable as they connect.

Start by jotting down anything on your mind. Don't worry about organization
at first — that comes later as patterns emerge.
